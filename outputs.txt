Regional carriers on capacity purchase agreements and ACMI cargo
operators, whose fuel a partner pays for. A different commercial
arrangement rather than a reporting error. The rows are retained.

                                    n  Median rate   Pooled R2   Within band       Spread
As published                     2175         2.43       0.922         0.689        0.039
Excluding under $1.00/gal        2043         2.45       0.986         0.862        0.036

R2 moves. The median rate and the spread do not. A measure built on
medians tolerates a mixed population; one built on squared deviations
does not.
